# Required Technology Website

Single-page marketing site whose only job is converting prospective B2B clients into contact-form submissions. Its design language is defined by ADR-0001.

## Language

### Design language

**Signature Accent**:
The one committed brand color, coral-red `#FF4438`, used as large solid surfaces and display elements — never as glows, gradient text, or small body text.
_Avoid_: primary color, brand blue, accent glow

**Hero Card**:
The giant rounded-rectangle surface inset from the viewport edges that opens the page, filled with a Signature Accent gradient and carrying the headline.
_Avoid_: hero section, banner, jumbotron

**Blueprint Frame**:
The drafting-idiom page chrome: dotted-grid texture in the page margins, 1px hairline rules bounding the content column, and square registration marks on card corners.
_Avoid_: grid overlay, decoration, borders

**Trust Band**:
The strip of real, verifiable numbers (stats) that fills the social-proof slot. Self-descriptive phrases ("Enterprise Ready") and fabricated logos/testimonials are banned from it.
_Avoid_: logo marquee, testimonials, trust indicators

**Ticker**:
The slow CSS-only scrolling strip of benefit statements inside the closing CTA card; the page's only ambient motion besides hover states.
_Avoid_: carousel, marquee (as a term; the effect is fine)

**Wordmark**:
The text-only brand mark: "Required Technology" in Schibsted Grotesk medium in the nav, spaced-caps "REQUIRED TECHNOLOGY" as the footer signature. There is no logo glyph.
_Avoid_: logo, brand mark

### Voice

**Plain-Confident**:
The copy register: short declarative claims a senior engineer would stand behind, warm but sober, at most one wink on the whole page.
_Avoid_: playful-parenthetical, agency-speak ("digital solutions", "bring your ideas to life")
