# Adopt a Cloudflare-derived design language with a coral-red signature accent

The site's original look was "glowing dark SaaS" (blue glows, blur orbs, gradient text) over a cool blue-black palette. We decided (2026-09-12) to rebuild the visual language heavily inspired by cloudflare.com's 2026 redesign — warm near-black ground (`#151414`), cream text, one committed accent in big solid blocks, blueprint hairline/dotted-grid chrome, pill buttons, grotesk display type at medium weight — while replacing their signature orange with our own coral-red `#FF4438` and rewriting copy to a plain-confident voice. Scope is full: re-skin, re-layout, and re-voice.

## Considered Options

- **Cloudflare's exact orange (`#FF5E1F`)** — rejected: copying the signature color of one of the most recognizable tech brands reads as a clone to exactly the technical audience we sell to.
- **Keeping the existing blue** — rejected: quietly cancels the warmth that motivated choosing Cloudflare as reference.
- **Amber (`#FFB000`)** — rejected: fails white-on-accent contrast for headline text inside the Hero Card.
- **FT Kunst Grotesk** (their typeface) — commercial license; substituted Schibsted Grotesk (Google Fonts) for display, Inter retained for body.

## Consequences

- The brand color changes from blue to coral-red across all touchpoints; anything external referencing the blue brand (email template accents, social cards) must follow.
- No social proof may be fabricated: the Trust Band carries only real numbers until real client logos/quotes exist.
- Dark-only remains; light mode is explicitly out of scope.
